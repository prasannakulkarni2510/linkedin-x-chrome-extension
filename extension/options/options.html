Pacing guard

LinkedIn and X restrict accounts that act faster than a person normally would. The guard slows down actions that change things on either site. One guard covers each open tab. Skipping and reading are never limited.

Diagnostics

If Swipe Mode can't find posts or buttons, the site probably changed its page. Open your LinkedIn feed or X timeline in a tab, then run this to see what Swipe Mode can detect. Nothing is sent anywhere.


    


    

      
Good to know

      
    
        
  • LinkedIn's help pages say third-party extensions that modify or automate LinkedIn are not allowed and can lead to account restrictions. Use Swipe Mode at your own risk.
    • 
        
  • X's automation rules prohibit automated likes, reposts and replies. Swipe Mode acts only when you pick an action, but X can still treat extension activity as automation. Use it at your own risk.
    • 
        
  • Swipe Mode keeps nothing after you exit: no history, no account, no server.
